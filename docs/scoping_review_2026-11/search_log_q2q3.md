# Search log — scoping review Q2/Q3 (protocol v0, 2026-11-08)

Screener: AI agent (single screener; protocol §4 deviation). Scope: concept blocks **Q2** (LLM knowledge of associational/causal structure among human-data variables, stated or implied through simulated respondents, incl. moderation) and **Q3** (cross-national/cultural/subgroup heterogeneity of that knowledge and whether it tracks *measured* heterogeneity; transfer across countries).
All searches run 2026-11-08 (UTC). Downloads are under `raw/` in this folder and were treated as untrusted data.

## Plain-language terms used here

- **Silicon sampling / simulated respondents**: an LLM is asked to answer a survey as a person with given characteristics; relationships between variables are then estimated from those synthetic answers ("implied" structure).
- **Stated structure**: the LLM is asked directly which variables relate/predict, or how strongly (no synthetic respondents).
- **Heterogeneity levels** (charting field, protocol §5): *none* = no group comparison; *levels* = averages or accuracy compared across groups/countries; *structure* = a relationship (slope, effect, importance, correlation) estimated per group and compared with that group's real one; *structure-vs-measured-heterogeneity* = the paper explicitly tests whether **between-group differences in a relationship** produced by the LLM match the **measured** between-group differences (e.g. correlating LLM and real per-country slopes, or comparing an LLM's owner–renter difference in an effect with the real difference). Strict rule applied: interactions between experimental factors alone do not count; the moderator must be a group (country, region, demographic subgroup).

## 5. Deviations and limitations

1. Single AI screener; bulk title exclusions and the keyword pre-screen of S2 citers are not dual-screened.
2. arXiv relevance ranking: only the first 400 hits of A (1,414) and B (5,459) and the first 300 of F04, F12, F13, F18 were screened.
3. Several journal records were charted from abstracts only (access=abstract); headline numbers for those come only from the abstract or from a search snippet, as marked in chart.csv.
4. Nine S2 records exist but have no abstract and no reachable full text; coded `E6*` (content unverifiable) for the PI to retrieve. Strict ARS rule: unconfirmed existence → E6; here existence is confirmed but content is not, so they are not charted.
